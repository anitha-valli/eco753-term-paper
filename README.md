# Term Paper - Labor Force Participation Across the Life Cycle for Indian Women (working title)

These are files for the ECO753: Labor Economics term paper, advised by Dr. Sounak Thakur, Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur.


## Abstract

This analysis was conducted by constructing synthetic cohorts using NSS and PLFS (repeated cross-sectional data). I focussed primarily on women born in 1980–84 and 1985–89, and track their LFPR across the survey rounds as the cohorts move through different age groups. Synthetic cohorts are constructed due to the unavailability of suitable longitudinal data in India. I also checked for variations in the trajectories of both the cohorts - the difference between the participation rate of the two cohorts was statistically insignificant.

The analysis accounts for rural/urban residence and I also hope to look at the effect of educational attainment in the future. 

The primary limitations are interpreting these trajectories as individual life-cycle patterns as well as differences in survey design between the NSS and PLFS.


The repository only contains the cleaning script for NSS55 data and the script with the regressions as a sample for now. I hope to upload the remaining files soon!
